Privacy Policy

Tamil Daily Bible Verse & Prayer Reminder is a devotional utility application.

Information Collection and Use

This application does not collect, transmit, or sell any personal identifiable information. Verse queries and user settings (such as theme preferences and saved verses) are processed locally on your device or via secure stateless requests.

Permissions

The app requires standard internet access to fetch daily scriptures and optional notification permissions to deliver local daily prayer reminders.
